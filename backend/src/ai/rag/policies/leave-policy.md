# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Leave types and annual allocation

Every confirmed employee receives the following allocation at the start of each calendar year:

- **Paid Time Off** — 24 days per year. Credited in full on 1 January. New joiners receive a pro-rated allocation of 2 days for each remaining full month of the year.
- **Sick Leave** — 7 days per year. Credited in full on 1 January and not pro-rated.
- **Unpaid Leave** — no fixed allocation. Granted at the discretion of the reporting manager and HR.

Paid Time Off and Sick Leave allocations are separate and cannot be exchanged for one another. Unused Sick Leave does not carry forward.

## Carry forward and encashment

Up to 8 unused Paid Time Off days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any balance above 8 days lapses on 31 December. Carried-forward days must be used before 31 March of the following year.

Leave encashment is not offered during employment. On resignation or termination, unused Paid Time Off up to a maximum of 8 days is paid out with the final settlement at basic salary rate. Sick Leave and Unpaid Leave are never encashed.

## Applying for leave

All leave requests are submitted through the Dayflow platform. A request must include the leave type, the start and end date, and a reason.

- **Paid Time Off** must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance. Requests covering more than 5 consecutive days require 10 working days notice.
- **Sick Leave** may be applied for retrospectively, on or after the day of absence.
- **Unpaid Leave** must be applied for at least 5 working days in advance.

Requests are reviewed by an HR officer, who may approve or reject them with a comment. Employees are notified of the decision in the platform. A request that has not been approved is not leave — absence without an approved request is recorded as absent.

## Medical certificates

A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for any Sick Leave absence of **3 or more consecutive days**. The certificate must be attached to the leave request in the platform. Sick Leave of 1 or 2 days does not require a certificate.

Absence of 3 or more days without a certificate is reclassified as Unpaid Leave and reduces payable days for the month.

## Public holidays and weekends

Public holidays and weekly offs falling inside a leave period are not counted against the leave balance. Only working days are deducted.

## Effect on salary

Approved Paid Time Off and Sick Leave do not reduce payable days and have no effect on monthly salary.

Unpaid Leave reduces the number of payable days for that month, which reduces net pay proportionally. Days marked absent without an approved leave request are treated the same as Unpaid Leave for payroll purposes.
