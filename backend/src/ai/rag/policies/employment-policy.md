# Employment, Probation and Exit Policy

## Probation

All new employees serve a probation period of **6 months** from the date of joining. Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, with written notice from HR.

During probation the notice period is **15 days** for either party. Employees on probation accrue Sick Leave normally but receive pro-rated Paid Time Off, credited at 2 days for each completed month of service.

Confirmation at the end of probation is confirmed in writing by HR. Employment terms, including the notice period, change to the confirmed-employee terms from the date of confirmation.

## Notice period

For confirmed employees the notice period is **60 days** for both resignation and termination, unless the employment contract states otherwise.

A resignation must be submitted in writing to the reporting manager and HR. The notice period begins on the date HR acknowledges the resignation, not the date it was written.

The company may waive part of the notice period at its discretion. An employee who wishes to leave earlier may request a buyout, in which case the shortfall is recovered from the final settlement at basic salary rate. Notice period shortfall cannot be offset against unused leave.

Paid Time Off may not be taken during the notice period except with written approval from HR. Sick Leave during notice requires a medical certificate regardless of duration.

## Final settlement

Final settlement is processed within **45 days** of the last working day. It includes salary for days worked, encashment of unused Paid Time Off up to 8 days at basic salary rate, and any pending reimbursements, less any recoveries.

Settlement is released only after all company assets have been returned and the exit clearance is complete.

## Provident Fund

Provident Fund is deducted at **12% of basic salary** from the employee, with a matching 12% employer contribution. Contributions are remitted to the employee's UAN.

On leaving, the PF balance may be transferred to the next employer using the UAN, or withdrawn subject to statutory conditions. HR does not process withdrawals; employees apply directly through the EPFO portal.

## Professional Tax

Professional Tax of **₹200 per month** is deducted from gross salary and remitted to the state authority. The amount is fixed and does not vary with salary.

## Reimbursements

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are incurred wholly for company work, supported by an original receipt, and submitted within **30 days** of being incurred.

Claims are submitted through the platform with the receipt attached, approved by the reporting manager, and paid with the following month's salary. Claims submitted more than 30 days after the expense date are not reimbursed except with written approval from HR.

Travel undertaken for company work is reimbursed at actuals for transport and accommodation, within the grade limits published by HR. Personal travel, fines and penalties are never reimbursed.

## Leave Travel Allowance

Leave Travel Allowance is a salary component paid monthly as part of the salary structure. It is calculated as 8.33% of basic salary. It is paid regardless of whether travel is undertaken and does not require receipts, though tax exemption on LTA requires proof of travel and is claimed by the employee at the time of filing.
