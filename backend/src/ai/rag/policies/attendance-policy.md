# Attendance and Working Hours Policy

## Standard working schedule

The standard working week is 5 days, Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs.

Standard working hours are 9 hours per day, of which 8 hours are working time and 1 hour is an unpaid break. Core hours, during which all employees are expected to be available, are 11:00 to 17:00.

## Check in and check out

Employees record attendance using the Check In and Check Out control in the Dayflow platform. Check In must be recorded at the start of the working day and Check Out at the end.

Only one attendance record exists per employee per calendar day. Forgetting to check out results in an incomplete record, which HR must correct manually. Repeated incomplete records are treated as an attendance issue.

## Attendance status types

Each day is recorded with exactly one status:

- **Present** — the employee checked in and completed at least 4 hours of work.
- **Half-day** — the employee checked in but completed fewer than 4 hours of work.
- **Leave** — an approved time-off request covers the day.
- **Absent** — no check in was recorded and no approved leave covers the day.

## Late arrival and short hours

Arrival after 10:30 without prior notice to the reporting manager is recorded as a late arrival. Three late arrivals in a calendar month are counted as one half-day.

A working day of fewer than 4 hours is recorded as a half-day. Two half-days are treated as one absent day for payroll purposes.

## Extra hours

Time worked beyond the standard 8 working hours is recorded as extra hours in the attendance record. Extra hours are tracked for visibility and workload planning. They do not accrue as compensatory time off and are not separately paid, unless a written overtime arrangement exists for the role.

## Attendance and payroll

Attendance records are the basis for payslip generation. The system uses attendance to determine the number of payable days for each employee in a month.

Unpaid leave and days marked absent reduce payable days. Approved Paid Time Off and Sick Leave do not. Net pay for the month is calculated in proportion to payable days against total working days.

## Corrections and disputes

An employee who believes an attendance record is wrong should raise it with HR within 7 days of the date in question. HR can amend records. Amendments made after payroll has been processed for that month are adjusted in the following month's payslip.

## Visibility

Employees can view only their own attendance records. HR officers and administrators can view attendance for all employees, including a company-wide view for any given day.
